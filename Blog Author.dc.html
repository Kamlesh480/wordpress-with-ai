ABOUT

Thirteen years of casting floors and municipal sites

Ramesh joined Shubh Sanskar Concrete in 2013 and now leads site quality control across our RCC hume pipe and manhole system deliveries. He has walked drainage lines from Indore to Bhopal, checked jointing on thousands of pipe sections, and signs off every batch before it leaves the yard. His writing comes from what actually goes wrong on site — and how to specify a product so it doesn't.

Ramesh holds a diploma in civil engineering and is trained on IS 458 and IS 1726 testing procedures. He works closely with contractors during specification, not just after delivery.

"Half the site problems we see were decided on paper, weeks before the pour."

EXPERTISE: RCC Hume Pipes Manhole Systems IS Code Compliance
